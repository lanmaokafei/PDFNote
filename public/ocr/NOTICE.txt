PDFNote offline OCR assets

- tesseract.js (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js
- tesseract.js-core WebAssembly builds (Apache-2.0): https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js-core
- chi_sim / eng traineddata from tessdata.projectnaptha.com 4.0.0 (Apache-2.0):
  https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata

On macOS and Windows, PDFNote prefers the operating system OCR engine
(Apple Vision / Windows.Media.Ocr). Tesseract.js is the offline fallback,
including Linux. No OCR request leaves the device.
